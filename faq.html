Pathum Dilshan – Frequently Asked Questions

Who is Pathum Dilshan?

Pathum Dilshan is a Sri Lankan engineer who works as an Engineer – Network Planning & Optimization at Mobitel (Pvt) Ltd, Colombo. He plans and optimises Mobitel's 2G/3G/4G/5G mobile network and builds data and machine learning tools for network KPIs. He is not the Sri Lankan cricketer of the same name.

What does Pathum Dilshan do at Mobitel?

He works on radio network planning and optimisation. This includes LTE capacity and IBS/DAS design for Bandaranaike International Airport, neighbour-list and PCI optimisation, LTE/VoLTE feature rollouts, and support for a 500+ site Huawei-to-ZTE vendor swap. He also builds Python and Apache Airflow pipelines that process more than 10 million KPI records a day.

What are his main results?

Across his optimisation work: call drop rate reduced by 15% and downlink throughput increased by 20% across 250+ sites, handover success improved by 12%, manual reporting effort cut by about 40% through automation, and anomaly detection accuracy improved by 18% compared with static thresholds.

What kinds of roles is he open to?

Network and RF engineering, IT and network infrastructure management, operations and project management, and data and AI roles in telecom and technology, in Sri Lanka or remotely for international companies.

What does an RF and network planning engineer do?

An RF (radio frequency) and network planning engineer designs where and how a mobile network provides coverage and capacity. This means choosing sites, frequency bands and antenna configurations, then optimising parameters such as neighbour lists, PCIs and handover settings, so that calls, data and video work reliably as users move.
