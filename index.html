About Pathum Dilshan

Pathum Dilshan is a telecommunications engineer from Colombo, Sri Lanka. He works as an Engineer – Network Planning & Optimization at Mobitel, where he plans and optimizes a live GSM, UMTS, LTE and 5G network and builds data tools that make that work faster.

He holds a BSc (Hons) in Electronic & Telecommunication Engineering from CINEC Campus and is reading for an MSc in Data Science & Artificial Intelligence at the University of Moratuwa. His research focuses on drift-aware anomaly detection and root-cause ranking for mobile RAN KPI data.

He is open to RF/RAN optimization, telecom data & AI, network analytics and data engineering roles, on-site in the UAE, Saudi Arabia, Qatar or Malaysia, or remote.
